Living in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar or another country far from home often means missing birthdays, anniversaries and festivals in Kerala. A gift can close some of that distance, but only if it feels personal. This guide explains how to send a handmade crochet gift to your family in Kerala from abroad, step by step, using WhatsApp.

Everything here is based on how BloomyCocoon works today. We are a handmade crochet gift brand in Mampad, Malappuram, Kerala, and every piece is made to order.

## Start with the person and the occasion

Before you look at any product, think about who the gift is for and what the moment is. A bouquet suits an anniversary. A plushie and a flower suit a birthday. A hamper suits a new baby or a family celebration. When you know the person and the occasion, the choice becomes much easier.

If you would like ideas, browse our page on [sending gifts to Kerala](/send-gifts-to-kerala), or our wider range of [crochet gifts in Kerala](/crochet-gifts-kerala). Both pages list gifts that suit family occasions, and you can also explore our bouquets, plushies, baby gifts and home decor.

## Pick a gift that is made to order

Every BloomyCocoon gift is crocheted by hand after you order. That is good news if you are far away, because it means the gift can be shaped around the person: the colours can suit them, and you can add a handwritten calligraphy note card with your own message. Warm fairy lights (with battery) can also be added to finish the gift.

Prices on the site are shown as "Starting from". The final price is confirmed with you on WhatsApp, so you always know what you are paying before we start.

## Message us on WhatsApp from any number

You do not need an Indian number to order. You can message us on WhatsApp from any number, including a UAE or Saudi number. The simplest way is to add your gifts to the cart on the website and send the order to us on WhatsApp. If you would rather start with a conversation, just write to us and describe what you have in mind.

Tell us three things: what you would like to send, who it is for, and where in Kerala it needs to go. We deliver across Kerala and India, so wherever your family lives in Kerala, share the location on chat.

## What we confirm before we start

Before anything is made, we confirm four things with you on WhatsApp:

- the final price of the gift;
- the delivery charge, which is not fixed and depends on the delivery;
- the date, so you know when to expect it;
- the colours and any personal details, such as a note card message.

Making begins only after this is clear. If you change your mind about a colour or a detail, tell us before we start.

## How you pay

Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp, the standard payment is a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay. If you are paying from abroad, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm a payment method that works for you.

The balance is paid later, after you have approved the finished gift. You never pay the rest before you have seen the result.

## Approve a photo before dispatch

When your gift is finished, we send you a photo of it on WhatsApp so you can approve it. Only after you approve do you pay the balance, and only then do we dispatch the gift. This step matters when you are not there in person: you see the gift before it goes to your family.

## How early to order

Every gift is made by hand and delivered in 1-2 weeks. For birthdays, anniversaries, Onam, Vishu and Eid, message us at least 2 weeks before the day. That gives us time to confirm the details, make the gift with care and send it. If you are planning for Valentine's Day, see our [Valentine's Day gifts](/valentines-day-gifts) and plan ahead in the same way.

## A short checklist

- Choose the person and the occasion.
- Pick a gift, or describe your idea on WhatsApp.
- Message us from any number, including a Gulf number.
- Share the delivery location in Kerala.
- Confirm the final price, delivery charge, date and colours.
- Pay the 50% advance after confirmation.
- Approve the photo of the finished gift.
- Pay the balance, and we dispatch your gift.
