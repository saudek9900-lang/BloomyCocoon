## What "Starting from" means

Every price on this page is a "Starting from" price. It tells you where a piece begins, not the final amount you will pay. The final price for your bouquet is confirmed with you on WhatsApp, based on your choices, before we start making anything.

The delivery charge is not fixed either. We confirm it with you on WhatsApp before making begins, so there are no surprises later.

## Why handmade costs more

A crochet bouquet is stitched one flower at a time, by hand, and only after you place your order. That slow, made-to-order work is what you are paying for. It is also why we meet your colour and flower choices on WhatsApp first, rather than sending something off a shelf.

Our range has two kinds of pieces, and the price difference comes from what you are gifting.

## How ordering works

Ordering a bouquet takes six simple steps:

1. Add your gifts to the cart.
2. Send your order to us on WhatsApp.
3. We confirm the final price, delivery charge and date with you.
4. Pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay.
5. We send you a photo of your finished gift to approve.
6. Pay the balance, and we dispatch your gift.

Your bouquet is made and delivered in 1-2 weeks. The 50% advance is only asked for after we have confirmed your order on WhatsApp, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before paying the balance and before it is dispatched.

## Choosing the right bouquet for your budget

A few simple ways to decide:

- **Start with the occasion.** For a quick thank-you or a small surprise, a single stem is enough. For an anniversary or a birthday where you want a bigger moment, choose a bouquet.
- **Think about colour.** Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp, so tell us the shades you like, or the palette of the room or event.
- **Plan the timing.** Because every bouquet is made by hand, message us at least two weeks before the date you need it.
- **Add a personal touch.** A calligraphy card with your own message turns a bouquet into something that feels written just for the person receiving it.
