The Crochet Plant Holder is a cosy plant holder for shelves, windows and handmade home decor gifts. It is crocheted by hand in soft yarn and carries a Kept Favorite badge.

It suits plant lovers, housewarming gifts and anyone who likes a soft, handmade touch around their plants. It sits well on a shelf or in a window.

Tell us the colours you would like on WhatsApp, and we confirm the shades with you before making begins. If you have a particular plant in mind, tell us and we confirm the details together.

Keep it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture, and avoid prolonged direct sunlight so the colours stay lovely. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
