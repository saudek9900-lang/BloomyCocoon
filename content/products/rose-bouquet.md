The Rose Bouquet is a handmade crochet bouquet of roses, wrapped as a gift. Each flower is stitched slowly by hand in soft yarn, so the bouquet is made to be kept rather than thrown away.

It is a natural choice for anniversaries and couple gifts, and it is one of the gifts we list for Valentine's Day. A crochet bouquet also suits soft surprises, simply as a way to say you were thinking of someone.

Colours and wrapping are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we start, so the bouquet can match the person you are gifting and the occasion. A handwritten calligraphy note card and warm fairy lights can be added if you want a little more to open.

A few gentle habits keep it lovely. Store it somewhere clean and dry, away from prolonged moisture, and dust it with a soft, dry brush. Avoid pulling at the stems or stitches.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
