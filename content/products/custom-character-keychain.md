The Custom Character Keychain is a character-inspired charm built from your own idea or theme. Instead of choosing a ready-made design, you tell us what you want and we shape the keychain around it.

It suits people who want a charm that looks like something specific: a favourite character, a theme for an event or an idea that only makes sense to the person receiving it. It also works as a personal add-on to a larger hamper.

Because the design is custom, the final details are confirmed on WhatsApp before making begins. Tell us your idea, the colours and the theme, and we agree what the finished keychain will look like.

To keep the colour bright, keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight. For light dust, a soft, dry brush or a gentle shake outdoors is enough.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
