The Mini Teddy Plushie is a small handmade teddy bear, crocheted in soft yarn with a friendly, huggable face. It is the kind of gift that can sit on a desk, a shelf or a bedside table and quietly make the day better.

It is made for cute birthday gifts, student desks and tiny keepsakes, and it appears on our birthday gifts page. It also works well beside a flower or a note card, which is an easy way to turn it into a complete small gift.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we begin, so you can choose a teddy in a favourite shade or a colour that matches someone's room. A handwritten calligraphy note card can be added.

To keep your plushie in shape, store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush and avoid pulling at the stitches or any attached details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
