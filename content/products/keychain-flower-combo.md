The Keychain + Flower Combo is a small gift pairing: a handmade keychain together with a handmade flower. It is two small pieces made to be given together.

It suits students, friends and surprise notes, and we list it with our return gifts. It is also a simple way to give something that feels like a complete gift without a big box.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making begins, so the keychain and the flower can match each other. A handwritten note card can be added to finish the gift.

Each item in the set is handmade, so handle it gently. Store everything in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
