They suit gifts for friends and family, festive outfits and anyone who likes a handmade accessory. They also work as an accessory to wrap up with a note card.

Keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight to protect the colour, and avoid placing heavy objects on it. Delicate accessories keep their shape when they are stored with care.
