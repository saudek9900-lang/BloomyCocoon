The Initial Letter Keychain is a personal charm made around a single letter, crocheted by hand in yarn. Its badge says Personalized, because the whole point is that it is made for one person.

It suits names, students, couples and handmade gift hampers: a letter for a friend, one for each person in a pair or an initial that completes a larger gift. It is a small piece that feels thought through.

Tell us the letter and the colours you want on WhatsApp, and we confirm the details with you before we start. If you are ordering for a couple, tell us on WhatsApp and we plan the pieces together.

Avoid placing heavy objects on it in a bag or drawer so it keeps its shape. Brush off light dust with a soft, dry brush and handle it gently.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
