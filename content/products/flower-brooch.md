The Flower Brooch is a crochet flower that can be pinned to bags, sarees, jackets and gift wraps. It is a small piece of handmade colour that can change an outfit or finish a present.

It suits gifts for friends and family, festive outfits and anyone who likes to add a flower to a favourite jacket or saree. It is also a pretty way to decorate a wrapped gift.

Tell us the colours you would like on WhatsApp and we confirm the shades with you before making begins. A handwritten note card can be added if you are giving it as a gift.

If you are not sure how to clean it, ask us before washing it. In daily use, gentle handling helps preserve its shape, finishing and small details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
