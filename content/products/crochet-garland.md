The Crochet Garland is a handmade garland for rooms, celebrations, nursery corners and gentle festive decor. It is crocheted in soft yarn and made to hang across a wall, a shelf or a window.

It suits birthdays, small parties, nursery corners and festive rooms, and it is an easy way to add handmade colour to a space without a large piece of decor. It also works as a housewarming gift.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for a palette that suits the room or the celebration. We confirm the details with you before making begins.

Every piece is handmade, so gentle handling matters. Keep it dry and out of harsh sunlight, and message us for care advice about your specific piece.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
