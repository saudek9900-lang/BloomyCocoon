The Octopus Plushie is a tiny handmade octopus with soft tentacles and cheerful detail, crocheted in soft yarn. Its soft tentacles give it a charming, slightly silly look.

It works as a quirky gift for friends who like unusual plushies, as a desk companion or as a small surprise tucked into a hamper. It is also an easy gift if you are looking for something a little different.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making starts, so you can ask for a calm blue, a bright pink or any palette that fits the person. A handwritten note card can be added if you want a message to go with it.

To keep your plushie in shape, store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush and avoid pulling at the stitches or any attached details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
