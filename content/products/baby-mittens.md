Baby Mittens are tiny handmade mittens, stitched for comfort and softness and made for newborn gifting. They are small pieces, so they are worked slowly and carefully by hand.

They suit baby showers and newborn hampers, and we list them on our baby shower gifts page. They are a lovely small gift on their own, and they also go well beside a cap or booties.

Colours and sizing are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we start, because there are no standard sizes. Tell us the colour you would like and we confirm everything together.

For light dust, use a soft, dry brush or gently shake it outdoors. Keeping it dry helps it stay soft and neat.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
