Mini Pocket Plushies are small handmade plushies made for tiny gifting, in soft yarn and with plenty of charm for their size. They are small pocket plushies, made with tiny gifting in mind.

They suit students, desk friends and surprise add-ons, and we list them with our return gifts, where a small plushie makes a memorable favour. They also work as a sweet extra inside a hamper.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making begins. If you are planning a party or a celebration, tell us the quantity you need and we confirm the final details together.

Handle it with care so the shape and the small details stay as they were stitched. For light dust, shake it gently outdoors, and keep it dry.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
