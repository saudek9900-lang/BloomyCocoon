The Door Hanging is a soft crochet door hanging for festive, nursery or everyday decor. It is made by hand in soft yarn and meant to bring a gentle, handmade welcome to a doorway.

It suits festive homes, nursery doors and everyday rooms that could use a little colour. It is also a thoughtful gift for housewarmings or for someone with a new baby.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for shades that suit the season or the room. We confirm everything before making begins.

Avoid placing heavy objects on it and avoid pulling at the stitches or attached details. When in doubt about cleaning, ask us before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
