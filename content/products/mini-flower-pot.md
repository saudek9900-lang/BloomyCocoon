The Mini Flower Pot is a mini crochet flower pot for desks, shelves and small decor corners. Both the flower and the pot are made by hand in soft yarn, so it is a little garden that needs no watering.

It suits desks, windowsills and shelves, and it works as a small housewarming gift or a cheerful present for someone who loves flowers but not the upkeep. It also makes a pleasant addition to a hamper.

Tell us your favourite colours on WhatsApp, and we confirm the shades with you before making begins. A handwritten note card can be added if you are giving it as a gift.

Avoid placing heavy objects on it and avoid pulling at the stitches or attached details. When in doubt about cleaning, ask us before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
