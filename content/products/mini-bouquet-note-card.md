The Mini Bouquet + Note Card is a mini crochet bouquet paired with a note card, made as a small gift that carries a message. It is the combo to choose when the words matter as much as the flowers.

It suits birthdays, apologies and sweet surprise gifts, and we list it on our birthday gifts page and our Valentine's Day page. It is also a gentle way to say thank you.

Tell us the colours you would like on WhatsApp, and share the message you want on the note card. We confirm the details with you before making begins.

Keep the pieces away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on them. For light dust, use a soft, dry brush and avoid pulling at the stitches.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
