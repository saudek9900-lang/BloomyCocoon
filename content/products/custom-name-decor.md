Custom Name Decor is a personalised crochet name decor piece for rooms, doors, baby gifting and keepsakes. Its Name Custom badge says the main idea: the piece is made around the name you choose.

It suits a child's room, a nursery door, a desk or a corner reserved for a keepsake, and it makes a gift that is clearly meant for one person. It is also a good way to mark a new baby or a new home.

Tell us the name and the colours you want on WhatsApp, and we confirm the details with you before we begin. Because every piece is personal, the final design is agreed together before stitching starts.

For light dust, gently shake it outdoors or use a soft, dry brush. Handle it with care to preserve its shape, finishing and small details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
