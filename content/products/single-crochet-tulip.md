The Single Crochet Tulip is one cheerful tulip on its own stem, worked in soft yarn by hand and made to be kept. It is stitched in Mampad, Kerala, one stem at a time.

Because it stands alone, it fits birthdays, desk corners and surprises that do not need a full bouquet. We also list it among our return gifts, where a single stem makes a simple, memorable favour.

Shade choices are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making begins, so you can match the tulip to a favourite colour, a card or a small hamper. Add a note card if you want a message to travel with it.

To keep the colours lovely, keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on the flowers. For light dust, gently shake it outdoors rather than rubbing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
