The Wall Hanging is a crochet wall piece made to warm up rooms, doors and blank walls. It carries a Boho Premium badge, and it is crocheted by hand in soft yarn.

It suits living rooms, bedrooms and nursery walls, and it makes a thoughtful housewarming or room gift. It is the kind of decor piece that turns a plain wall into something personal.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for a palette that suits the wall and the room. We confirm the details with you before making begins.

Avoid placing heavy objects on it and avoid pulling at the stitches or attached details. When in doubt about cleaning, ask us before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
