The Custom Animal Plushie is built from your own idea. You describe the animal you have in mind, we agree the details with you, and the plushie is stitched slowly by hand in soft yarn.

It is a fine choice when a ready-made plushie is not quite right: a favourite pet, a character from a story or an animal with a special meaning. It is also listed on our birthday gifts page, where a personal plushie often becomes the main gift.

Size, colours and the final quote are all confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we begin, so you know exactly what you are getting. Tell us your idea in as much detail as you like; it helps us match it.

Keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on top of it, which can squash its shape. Not sure how to clean your plushie? Ask us before you wash it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
