The Penguin Plushie is a rounded handmade penguin, crocheted in soft yarn and made for tiny shelves and warm gifting. Its simple, rounded shape is easy to love at first sight.

It makes a thoughtful present for someone who loves penguins, and it is just as welcome as a small surprise alongside a larger gift.

Colour details are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we start, so you can tell us the palette you would like. Every penguin is stitched by hand in Mampad, Kerala.

Handle it with care so the shape and the small details stay as they were stitched. For light dust, shake it gently outdoors, and keep it dry.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
