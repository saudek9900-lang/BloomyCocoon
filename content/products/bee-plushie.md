The Bee Plushie is a sweet, mini handmade bee, crocheted in soft yarn for playful gifting and decor accents. Its stripes and cheerful look make it lively on any surface.

Use it as a small gift for a friend, a cheerful accent on a shelf or a surprise slipped into a bigger hamper. It is a good choice when you want something light-hearted instead of formal.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we start, so if you would like a different palette for the stripes, ask us and we will agree the shades with you first. A note card can be added to finish the gift.

To keep your plushie in shape, store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush and avoid pulling at the stitches or any attached details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
