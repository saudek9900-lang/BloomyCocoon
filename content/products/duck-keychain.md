The Duck Keychain is a cheerful crochet duck, bringing playful handmade charm to keys, pouches and small gifting moments. It is a small, happy accessory that is easy to spot on a bag.

It suits friends who like a little humour in their accessories, and it is a simple gift for any day when you want to give something small. It also works well as a sweet extra in a gift box.

Confirm your preferred yarn colours on WhatsApp before stitching begins. If you want it to sit well with other charms or a particular bag, tell us and we agree the shades with you first.

Keep it somewhere clean and dry, away from prolonged moisture. Not sure how to clean it? Ask us before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
