The Baby Cap is a cosy handmade cap for a baby, crocheted in soft yarn with custom colour and sizing options. Like all our baby pieces it is stitched to order, so it can be matched to the person it is for.

It is a thoughtful gift for baby showers and newborn gifting, and it is listed on our baby shower gifts page. It also pairs well with booties if you want to give a small set.

Colour and sizing are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we begin. There are no standard sizes, so tell us what you need and we agree the details with you first.

Keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on it, so it keeps its shape. If you are unsure how to clean it, ask us on WhatsApp before washing.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
