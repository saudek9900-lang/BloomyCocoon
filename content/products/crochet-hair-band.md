The Crochet Hair Band is a handmade hair band with a comfortable stretch and a cosy texture, worked in soft yarn. It carries a Trending badge.

It suits daily wear, gifts for friends and a little extra in a hamper of handmade accessories. It also goes well beside a scrunchie or a bow clip if you want to give a small set.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for a shade that suits the person or an outfit. We confirm the colours before we start stitching.

Store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. For light dust, a soft, dry brush or a gentle shake outdoors works well.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
