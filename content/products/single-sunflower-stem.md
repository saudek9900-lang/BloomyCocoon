The Single Sunflower Stem brings one bright, sunny flower to a space, crocheted by hand in soft yarn. It is made to be kept, so it can stay on display as long as you like.

It is made for desk corners, jars and small surprise gifts, and it appears on our birthday gifts page for those moments when a cheerful single flower is the right size of present. A sunflower also makes a thoughtful extra to tuck beside a larger gift.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so tell us the mood you want and we confirm the shades before we begin. It is stitched to order in Mampad, Kerala.

Handle it gently, because every flower is handmade. Keep it dry, avoid tugging at the stems, and if you are unsure how to clean a particular piece, ask us on WhatsApp before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
