The Dinosaur Plushie is a friendly handmade dinosaur with handmade spikes and custom colours, crocheted in soft yarn. It has all the fun of a dinosaur with none of the scary parts.

It is a playful gift for dinosaur fans of any age, and it looks great on a desk, a shelf or a bed. It is also an easy way to add something cheerful to a birthday hamper.

Tell us your favourite colours on WhatsApp and we confirm the shades with you before we begin. That way the dinosaur suits the person you are gifting.

A soft, dry brush is the gentlest way to dust it. Avoid tugging at the stitches, keep it out of prolonged moisture, and message us if you are not sure how to clean it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
