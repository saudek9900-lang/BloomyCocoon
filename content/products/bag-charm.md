The Bag Charm is a playful handmade charm for tote bags, backpacks and custom hampers. It is crocheted in soft yarn and made to hang where it can be seen.

It suits students, friends and anyone who likes to personalise a bag, and it is a good small add-on to a bigger gift or hamper. It also pairs well with a crochet tote bag.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for the shades that suit the bag or the person. We confirm everything before we begin stitching.

Store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. For light dust, a soft, dry brush or a gentle shake outdoors works well.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
