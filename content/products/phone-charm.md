The Phone Charm is a petite crochet charm crafted from soft handmade yarn for personal daily styling and cute gift add-ons. It is a small way to make a phone look like yours.

It suits students, friends and anyone who likes a small handmade detail on their phone, and it works as a tiny add-on to a larger gift. It also pairs well with a crochet phone sleeve.

Select your favourite shade combination on WhatsApp before making starts. We confirm the colours with you, so the finished charm matches the phone, the case or the person you are gifting.

If you are not sure how to clean it, ask us before washing it. In daily use, gentle handling helps preserve its shape, finishing and small details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
