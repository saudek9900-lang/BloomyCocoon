The Table Decor Piece is a handcrafted decor accent for coffee tables, shelves and gifting. It is crocheted by hand in soft yarn, and it is made to be seen, not hidden away in a drawer.

It suits housewarming gifts, coffee tables and shelves, and it is a good gift for someone who likes soft, handmade touches at home. It can also sit on a desk or a side table.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making begins, so the piece can match a room or a theme. Tell us the shades you like and we plan the piece with you.

Keep it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture, and avoid prolonged direct sunlight so the colours stay lovely. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
