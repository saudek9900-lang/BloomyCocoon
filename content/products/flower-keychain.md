The Flower Keychain is a cheerful crochet flower charm, stitched from soft handmade yarn for bags, keys and small gifts. It brings a bit of colour to things you use every day.

It suits friends, students and anyone who likes a little flower on their bag, and we list it with our return gifts, where a small charm makes a simple favour. It also works as a tiny extra inside a gift hamper.

Pick your preferred yarn shade on WhatsApp, whether you want to match a favourite colour or the rest of a hamper. We confirm the shade with you before we begin stitching.

To keep the colour bright, keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight. For light dust, a soft, dry brush or a gentle shake outdoors is enough.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
