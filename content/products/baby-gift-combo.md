The Baby Gift Combo is a sweet baby gift combo with plush, accessory or nursery elements, finalised through WhatsApp. It brings a few handmade pieces together so that a single gift feels generous.

We list it on our baby shower gifts page, and it suits baby showers, newborn visits and family gifting. It is a good choice when you want to give more than one piece without building a hamper from scratch.

Because the contents are finalised through WhatsApp, you can tell us what you would like in the combo, and we agree the pieces and the colours with you before making begins. Sizing for any wearable piece is confirmed with you too.

Each item in the set is handmade, so handle it gently. Store everything in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
