The Frog Plushie is a playful handmade frog with custom expressions and cosy colours, crocheted in soft yarn. Its expressive face is the main attraction, and each frog gets a little personality of its own.

It suits anyone who enjoys something a little different from the usual bunny or bear: a friend with a sense of humour, a desk that needs cheering up or a surprise for a birthday. It also looks good on a shelf in a bright room.

Tell us the expression and the colours you would like on WhatsApp, and we confirm the details with you before making begins. Because every frog is stitched to order, small changes are part of the process.

Keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on top of it, which can squash its shape. Not sure how to clean your plushie? Ask us before you wash it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
