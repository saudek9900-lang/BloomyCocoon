The Single Crochet Rose is exactly what its name says: one handmade rose on a single stem, crocheted in soft yarn and made to be kept. It is the simplest way to give a BloomyCocoon flower, stitched slowly to order in Mampad, Kerala.

A single stem suits the small gestures: a quiet thank-you, a student gift, a little surprise on Valentine's Day, or a favour handed out at a celebration. It is listed with our Valentine's Day gifts and our return gifts, and it sits happily beside a handwritten note card.

Tell us the colour you have in mind on WhatsApp and we confirm the shade with you before we start. If you would like the gesture to feel more personal, a note card carrying your own message can be added.

A few gentle habits keep it lovely. Store it somewhere clean and dry, away from prolonged moisture, and dust it with a soft, dry brush. Avoid pulling at the stems or stitches.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
