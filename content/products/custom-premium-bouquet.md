The Custom Premium Bouquet is made to your own idea. Instead of choosing a ready-made bouquet, you tell us the flower type, the palette, the size and the packaging you want, and we build the bouquet around that.

It is the one to pick for a milestone anniversary, a Valentine's Day surprise or any occasion where an ordinary bouquet would not say enough. We list it on both our anniversary and Valentine's Day pages.

Because every detail is personal, the conversation happens on WhatsApp: send us your idea, and we confirm the final design and the final quote together before making begins. A handwritten note card can be added to carry your message.

A few gentle habits keep it lovely. Store it somewhere clean and dry, away from prolonged moisture, and dust it with a soft, dry brush. Avoid pulling at the stems or stitches.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
