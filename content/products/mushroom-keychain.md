The Mushroom Keychain is a whimsical little mushroom, crocheted by hand and made for playful bag and key styling. It is a quirky choice for people who like their accessories to look a little storybook.

It makes a fun gift for friends, a small surprise for a birthday or an extra piece in a hamper of handmade charms. It is a good choice when you want something a little different from the usual flower or heart.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for the colours you like or something that matches a bag. We confirm the shades with you before making begins.

Keep it somewhere clean and dry, away from prolonged moisture. Not sure how to clean it? Ask us before washing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
