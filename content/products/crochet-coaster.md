The Crochet Coaster is a single handmade coaster for mugs, desks and cosy corners, crocheted by hand in soft yarn. It is a small piece of decor that gets used every day.

It suits housewarming gifts, desk setups and tea-time tables, and we list it with our return gifts, where a coaster makes a useful favour. If you want a set, you can order more than one and tell us what you need.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for shades that suit a table, a desk or a person. We confirm everything before making begins.

Keep it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture, and avoid prolonged direct sunlight so the colours stay lovely. Dust it gently with a soft, dry brush.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
