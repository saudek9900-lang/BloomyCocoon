The Teddy Keychain is a tiny handmade teddy, finished in soft yarn and made for everyday keys, backpacks and sweet surprise gifting. It is a small version of the classic bear that is easy to carry everywhere.

It suits students, friends and anyone who likes a little bear hanging from their bag. It is also a thoughtful extra to tuck into a hamper, a card or a larger gift.

Custom yarn colour choices are confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp before crafting begins. If you are giving it as part of a set, tell us the colours you are working with so we can match them.

Avoid placing heavy objects on it in a bag or drawer so it keeps its shape. Brush off light dust with a soft, dry brush and handle it gently.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
