The Couple Keychain Set is a coordinated pair of handmade keychains, made to be given and carried together. The two pieces are designed as a set, so they look right side by side.

It is made for couple gifts, friendship surprises and anniversary add-ons, and we list it on both our anniversary and Valentine's Day pages. One keychain can go to each person, or you can keep one and send the other.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before making begins, so the pair can follow a colour you both like. A note card can be added if you want the set to arrive with a message.

Handle it gently, since every charm is handmade. Keep it dry, and avoid pulling at the stitches or any attached details when it hangs from your keys or bag.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
