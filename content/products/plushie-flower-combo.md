The Plushie + Flower Combo is a cosy plushie paired with a handmade flower, made to be given together. It combines something to hold with something to display, which is why it feels like a complete gift.

It suits birthdays, couple gifts and warm occasions, and we list it on both our birthday gifts and Valentine's Day pages. It is a good choice when a flower alone feels too small and a plushie alone feels too plain.

Colours are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we start, so the plushie and the flower can match. A handwritten note card or warm fairy lights can be added to finish the gift.

If you are unsure how to clean any piece in the set, ask us before washing it. A soft, dry brush or a gentle shake outdoors is the gentlest way to dust it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
