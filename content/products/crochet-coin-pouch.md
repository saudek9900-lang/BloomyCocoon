The Crochet Coin Pouch is a compact pouch for cards, coins and small essentials, crocheted by hand in soft yarn. It carries a Retro Charm badge.

It suits daily use, small gifts for friends and family, and hampers that need a practical piece. It is a useful present for someone who likes to keep small things in one place.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so you can ask for shades that suit the person. We confirm the colours before making begins.

Handle it gently, because every piece is handmade. Keep it dry, and avoid pulling at the stitches or any attached details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
