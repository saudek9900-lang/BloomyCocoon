The Tulip Bouquet is a graceful handmade bouquet of crochet tulips, made in soft yarn and finished as a gift. Its gentle, graceful look makes it a calm alternative to a bouquet of roses.

It suits birthdays, thank-you gifts and quieter celebrations, and we also list it on our anniversary gifts page. If you want a bouquet that feels fresh and spring-like, this is the one to start with.

Tell us your preferred colours on WhatsApp and we confirm everything with you before making begins. A note card with your own words can be added, so the bouquet arrives with a message as well as flowers.

To keep the colours lovely, keep it away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on the flowers. For light dust, gently shake it outdoors rather than rubbing it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
