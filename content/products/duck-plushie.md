The Duck Plushie is a cheerful handmade duck with rounded details and a soft yarn texture. It has a bright, friendly look that makes it feel playful the moment you take it out of the packaging.

It makes an easy gift for friends, siblings or anyone who likes a small, happy figure on a shelf. Pair it with a handwritten note and it becomes a complete present without needing anything else.

Yarn and colours are chosen with you on WhatsApp, so tell us the shades you like and we agree them with you first. Every duck is stitched to order in Mampad, Kerala, so there is time to get the colours right before we begin.

A soft, dry brush is the gentlest way to dust it. Avoid tugging at the stitches, keep it out of prolonged moisture, and message us if you are not sure how to clean it.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
