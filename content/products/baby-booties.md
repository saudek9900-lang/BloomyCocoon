Baby Booties are soft crochet booties made for newborn gifting and baby showers. They are stitched slowly by hand in gentle yarn, one pair at a time, in Mampad, Kerala.

They suit baby showers, newborn gifts and newborn hampers, and we list them on our baby shower gifts page. A pair of booties is one of the simplest, sweetest things to give to new parents.

Gentle shades are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before we begin, and there are no standard sizes: sizing is confirmed with you to fit the little one. If you would like to add a matching piece, ask about the booties and cap set.

Handle it gently, because it is handmade. Store it in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture, and avoid pulling at the stitches or attached details.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1-2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
