The Sunflower Bouquet is a bright, cheerful handmade bouquet of crochet sunflowers, stitched slowly in soft yarn and wrapped as a gift. With golden yellow petals and warm brown centres, it brings sunny warmth that lasts without drooping or wilting.

It suits birthdays, congratulations, thank-you gifts, and cheerful surprises. We also feature it on our birthday gifts page as a joyful centrepiece gift for friends, partners, or siblings who love vibrant flowers.

Preferred flower colours and wrapping styles are confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp before making begins. A handwritten calligraphy note card can be added so your personal message accompanies the bouquet.

To keep your bouquet looking its best, display it away from prolonged direct sunlight and humid spots. For light dusting, a gentle shake outdoors or a soft dry brush is all it needs.

Every piece is made to order and delivered in 1–2 weeks: we confirm the final price and delivery charge with you on WhatsApp before making begins, you pay a 50% advance by UPI or Google Pay, and you approve a photo of the finished gift before you pay the balance and we dispatch it.
