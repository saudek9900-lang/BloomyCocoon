Our Story

BloomyCocoon is run by sisters from their home in Mampad, Kerala. What started as a cozy afternoon project has blossomed into a shared passion and a dedication to the slow, gentle rhythm of handcrafted crochet.

BloomyCocoon is a handmade crochet gifting brand from Mampad, Malappuram, Kerala, India, offering custom crochet bouquets, plushies, keychains, accessories, home decor, and personalized handmade gifts through WhatsApp-based ordering.

Working side-by-side from our home, we believe that in an age of instant gratification, there is profound beauty in the slow. Every piece we create is driven by the belief that handmade gifts carry an emotional warmth and a tactile history that mass production can never replicate. When you hold a BloomyCocoon creation, you're holding hours of our focused patience, mutual encouragement, and genuine care.

Crochet Care Guide

A few gentle habits to help your BloomyCocoon keepsake stay lovely for longer.

dry

Keep it dry

Store your crochet gift in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture. If you are unsure how to clean a particular piece, ask us before washing it.

cleaning_services

Dust gently

For light dust, use a soft, dry brush or gently shake the item outdoors. Avoid pulling at stitches, embroidery, attached details or bouquet stems.

wb_sunny

Protect colour and shape

Keep handmade pieces away from prolonged direct sunlight and avoid placing heavy objects on them, especially plushies, bouquets and delicate accessories.

volunteer_activism

Handle with care

Every BloomyCocoon piece is handmade. Gentle handling helps preserve its shape, finishing and small details for years of gifting and keepsake moments.

Care & Customization FAQs

How do I care for and clean this handcrafted crochet item? expand_more

Spot clean gently using a damp cloth and mild soap if needed. Avoid machine washing or harsh soaking. Allow to air dry in the shade to preserve the vibrant yarn colors and structural shape.

Can I request custom yarn colors or personal gift tags? expand_more

Yes! Every single piece is made slowly to order. Simply message us on WhatsApp (+91 9061174579) to select custom shades, personalized names, or handwritten calligraphy note cards.

How long does production and delivery take across Kerala and India? expand_more

Small pieces and standard bouquets take 3–5 working days to craft. Delivery within Kerala typically arrives in 2–3 days, and 4–7 days for other Indian states with live courier tracking.

How does payment work for custom handmade orders? expand_more

We require a 50% advance booking payment via UPI or Google Pay to begin hand-crocheting your order. Once completed, we send you high-resolution photos for approval before you pay the remaining balance and we dispatch with courier tracking.
